Villa Balbianello

Lake Como

The whole complex consists of two residential buildings, a church, and a portico (known as Loggia Durini) that can host weddings and events in a truly unique setting.

Castel del Monte

Puglia, Italy

An enigmatic octagonal castle built by Emperor Frederick II, blending Northern European, Islamic, and Classical architectural styles in perfect harmony.

Its unique shape and mathematical precision have made it a UNESCO World Heritage site. The castle sits atop a hill, offering panoramic views of the surrounding olive groves and the Adriatic Sea in the distance... View more

3 / 10

Château de Chambord

Loire Valley, France

The largest and most majestic of the Loire Valley châteaux, famous for its distinctive French Renaissance architecture and its stunning double-helix staircase.

Designed in part by Leonardo da Vinci, the castle features 440 rooms, 365 fireplaces, and an elaborate roof terrace that looks like a skyline of chimneys and turrets. The surrounding park is Europe's largest enclosed forest... View more

4 / 10

Neuschwanstein Castle

Bavaria, Germany

A 19th-century historicist palace on a rugged hill above the village of Hohenschwangau, built by King Ludwig II as a retreat and in homage to Richard Wagner.

The castle's fairy-tale appearance inspired Walt Disney's Sleeping Beauty Castle. Its interior is filled with lavish decorations, murals depicting scenes from Wagner's operas, and a spectacular view of the Alpine foothills... View more

5 / 10

Alhambra Palace

Granada, Spain

One of the most remarkable examples of Moorish architecture in the world, sitting atop the Sabika hill with a breathtaking view over the city of Granada.

The complex includes palaces, fortresses, and gardens, with intricate stucco work, geometric patterns, and serene courtyards. The Generalife gardens are a masterpiece of Islamic landscape design, with fountains and greenery that create an oasis of calm... View more

6 / 10

Mont Saint-Michel

Normandy, France

A stunning tidal island and abbey that rises dramatically from the sea, connected to the mainland by a causeway that is often covered by the tide.

The abbey church, built over centuries, is a marvel of medieval engineering. The narrow streets and winding staircases lead up to the top, where you can witness one of the most spectacular views in France. The surrounding bay is famous for its extreme tides... View more

7 / 10

Edinburgh Castle

Edinburgh, Scotland

A historic fortress that dominates the skyline of Edinburgh from its position atop Castle Rock, an extinct volcano that has been inhabited since the Iron Age.

The castle houses the Crown Jewels of Scotland, the Stone of Destiny, and the famous One o'Clock Gun. It has been a royal residence, a military garrison, and a prison, and today it is a symbol of Scottish resilience and pride... View more

8 / 10

Himeji Castle

Hyogo, Japan

Widely considered Japan's most beautiful surviving feudal castle, known for its brilliant white exterior and elegant, complex rooflines that resemble a great bird in flight.

The castle is a masterpiece of Japanese defensive architecture, with a network of gates, winding paths, and hidden defenses. It survived World War II bombings and the 1995 Kobe earthquake, making it a symbol of resilience and cultural heritage... View more

9 / 10

Bran Castle

Transylvania, Romania

Often associated with the legend of Dracula, this imposing fortress sits on a cliff between the Carpathian mountains, offering a mysterious and dramatic atmosphere.

Built in the 14th century, the castle served as a royal residence and a military stronghold. Its narrow staircases, secret passages, and collection of medieval art draw visitors from around the world who come to explore the myth and history of the region... View more

10 / 10

Prague Castle

Prague, Czech Republic

The largest ancient castle in the world, according to the Guinness Book of Records, encompassing a sprawling complex of palaces, churches, and gardens.

The castle has been the seat of Czech kings, Holy Roman emperors, and presidents. Highlights include the magnificent St. Vitus Cathedral, the Old Royal Palace, and Golden Lane, a charming street of tiny houses once inhabited by castle guards and goldsmiths... View more
